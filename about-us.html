Who We Are

A first-of-its-kind partnership bringing vocational education to secondary schools across Ondo State, Nigeria.

Our Story

The Multi-Skill Teenpreneurship Program — MSTP was born from a simple but powerful belief: that every young person in Nigeria deserves the chance to learn a skill that can change their life.

In May 2023, MSTP launched as a bold partnership between Lend-A-Hand India, ACCENT-Spring Initiative, Springboard, and the Ondo State Ministry of Education — bringing the proven Multi Skills Foundation Course (MSFC) to Africa for the very first time.

From nine pilot schools across Ondo State's three senatorial districts, MSTP is quietly transforming what it means to be a secondary school student in Nigeria.

Africa faces a significant challenge: universities produce thousands of graduates every year, yet youth unemployment remains high because academic qualifications alone cannot prepare young people for the realities of the modern economy.

MSTP exists to change that — one skill, one school, one student at a time.

Our Mission

"To equip secondary school students across Nigeria with vocational skills, entrepreneurial thinking, and the confidence to build self-reliant futures."

— MSTP Mission

Our Vision

"An Africa where every young person has access to a meaningful skill — and the opportunity to turn that skill into a livelihood."

— MSTP Vision

The MSTP Model

MSTP integrates five thematic skill areas into the junior secondary school curriculum: Wood, Electronics & Technology (WET); Electrical & Electronics (E&E); GNAT; Food Processing; and Introduction to Entrepreneurship.

Hands-On Practice

Students learn through hands-on practice — in workshops, labs, and gardens — mentored by trained instructors embedded in their own schools.

Annual Trade Fair

Each year, students showcase their work at the MSTP Trade Fair: a celebration of what young hands and minds can create, and a first taste of what it means to earn through skill.

The Founding Partnership

Lend-A-Hand India (LAHI)

Technical framework, curriculum, and knowledge transfer. LAHI's MSFC has been implemented in 500+ schools across India. MSTP is its first international adaptation.

ACCENT-Spring Initiative

On-the-ground coordination, stakeholder engagement, and operational management across Ondo State.

Springboard

Community development expertise, sustainable agriculture, and rural empowerment, enriching MSTP's vocational approach.

Ondo State Ministry of Education

Government endorsement, school access across all three senatorial districts, and institutional credibility for scale.
