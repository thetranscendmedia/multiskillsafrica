About Us

MSTP is a first-of-its-kind vocational and entrepreneurship programme for secondary school students in Ondo State, Nigeria. Since May 2023, we have partnered with schools, government, and global organisations to give young people the hands-on skills and business thinking they need to shape their own futures.

Read Our Story →
"Every young person in Africa has a skill inside them. MSTP's job is simply to find it — and to show them what it is worth."
— Mr Lawrence Afere Mentor, MSTP
What We Teach

Five hands-on skill modules, designed to turn secondary school students into capable, confident young Nigerians.

Module: WET

Workshop & Engineering Techniques

Identify tools and materials, construct basic wooden objects, and understand fundamental electronic principles.

Module: E&E

Energy & Environment

Foundations in electrical systems, wiring, and energy safety.

Module: GNAT

Gardening, Nursery & Agriculture Techniques

Grow food, understand nutrition, and participate in local trade.

Module

Food Processing Techniques

Food preservation, product development, basic food business knowledge..

Module

Introduction to Entrepreneurship

The MSTP Internship—students display and sell products they have made, earning real income for the first time..
