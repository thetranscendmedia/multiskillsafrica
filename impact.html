Student Stories

“

Blessing Adebayo

SS2 • Akingbala High School

Learning furniture and woodwork has completely changed how I think about my future after school. Before joining the MSTP workshop, I only had theoretical knowledge from textbooks, but now I can design and build actual wooden frames myself. The instructors make sure everyone understands the safety rules and techniques perfectly.

“

Ibrahim Musa

SS3 • Ondo Grammar School

The food processing module has given me practical skills that I can immediately use to help my family's business. I learned how to process, package, and safely preserve agricultural items without relying on expensive machinery. It feels great to create clean, market-ready packages all on our own during workshop hours.

“

Chnebogo Nwosu

SS2 • Community Secondary School

Fixing and maintaining electrical installations seemed impossible to me before this class started. Now I can troubleshoot simple domestic wiring setups and understand circuit components easily. These sessions have boosted my confidence and inspired me to study electrical engineering at the university level.
