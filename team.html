Board and Mentors

Akinwale Akinyanmi

MENTOR & LEAD, MSTP Africa

He is a Nigerian consultant who founded the ACCENT Entrepreneurship Development Initiative and focuses on social entrepreneurship, leadership, sustainability and innovation. He also mentors young people from a variety of backgrounds.

Lawrence Afere

MENTOR & LEAD, MSTP Africa

A social entrepreneur and farmer from Nigeria, who established Springboard. This organization co-facilitates MSTP Africa and works to empower rural women and small-scale farmers via equitable food production, sustainable living, cooperative movements, and the supply of high-quality cocoa beans.

Mrs. Bola Akinyanmi

MEMBER, Board of Trustees

She is a retired Permanent Secretary with 35 years of public service in Ondo State, Nigeria, where she pioneered environmental governance, developed the state's first Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation work plan, and initiated environmental policies and legislation at the state level. She holds a Master of Technology in Environmental Biology and Public Health, among other qualifications.

Pastor Akin Ibitokun

MEMBER, Board of Trustees

Mr. Akinola Ibitokun is a respected leader, governance expert, and development practitioner with a passion for building people and transforming communities. As Chairman of the Board of Springboard Farmers Co-operative Nigeria, he provides strategic leadership that advances farmer empowerment, cooperative growth, and sustainable agricultural development. He also serves as the Executive Director of Christian Heritage Ministries, championing values-based leadership and organizational excellence. Through decades of service, Mr. Ibitokun has remained committed to nurturing leaders, strengthening institutions, and creating sustainable impact
